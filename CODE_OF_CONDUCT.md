# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code without attacking
people. Respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
disruption and publication of private credentials are unacceptable in project
issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict
participation or ban participants. They should consider context, explain
decisions where safe, and avoid sharing private reports. A participant involved
in a complaint should not decide its outcome alone where another maintainer is
available.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for urgent platform abuse. For a project conduct
concern, contact a maintainer privately through an available GitHub channel;
never disclose sensitive details in a public issue. A dedicated private conduct
contact remains a release-readiness item. Security reports follow SECURITY.md.
